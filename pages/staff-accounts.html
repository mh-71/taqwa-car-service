Give a staff member their own sign-in, see who can sign in, and switch an account off when someone leaves.

How accounts work

  • An account lets one staff member sign in with their own username and password instead of the shared workshop passphrase, which keeps working.
  • Every account has full access (Administrator). Restricted roles such as Mechanic or Accountant need Roles & Permissions to be enforced first, so they cannot be given yet.
  • Deactivating an account, or setting a new password, signs that person out everywhere straight away. Someone whose staff record is inactive cannot sign in.
  • Password recovery by e-mail is not available. An administrator sets a new password here instead.

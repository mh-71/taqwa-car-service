How the activity log works

  • The server records an entry when a Staff Management change succeeds: staff added or edited, a user account created or changed (or its password set), attendance recorded or corrected, leave submitted, approved or rejected. A refused change is not recorded.
  • Each entry names who was signed in (an account, or the workshop passphrase), what was done and to which record. Passwords and other private details are never recorded; an edit lists the fields changed, not their values.
  • Entries cannot be edited or deleted. Sign-ins, page views and changes in other modules (customers, job cards, invoices, ...) are not recorded.

How attendance works

  • One record per staff member per day, for today or an earlier day. A day with no record shows as Not recorded — it is not counted as absent.
  • Check-in and check-out are workshop time, only for Present, Late or Half-day. A mistake is corrected with Edit; records are not deleted.
  • Nothing is calculated from attendance: no pay, overtime or leave balance.

Not Available with a Shared Account

Everyone signs in with the same workshop passphrase, so the system cannot tell staff apart. These need individual staff accounts, which this system does not have yet:

  • Personal name and photo
  • Changing your own password
  • Two-step verification
  • Sign-in history
  • Signing out other devices

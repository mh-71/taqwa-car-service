How performance is measured

  • Assigned counts the job cards whose job date falls in the period, by the mechanic on the job card; their status is the status now. Completed / Delivered, Open (Received, Inspection, Waiting for Approval, In Progress, Waiting for Parts) and Cancelled are the job card statuses as the Job Cards page uses them.
  • Completed in period counts the job cards whose completion time (stamped when a job card enters Completed) falls in the period, whatever their job date. On time compares that day with the job card's estimated delivery date, and only for job cards that have one.
  • Days are workshop days (Asia/Dhaka). Only mechanics are assigned job cards. Attendance, pay and customer feedback are not used, and no score or ranking is calculated. This page changes nothing.

"From" must be on or before "To".

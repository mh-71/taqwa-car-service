Leave requests and their decisions.

How leave requests work

  • A request names one active staff member, a leave type (Casual, Sick, Annual or Unpaid), the first and last day and a reason. It may not overlap another Pending or Approved request of the same person.
  • An administrator approves or rejects a pending request once; a rejection says why. A decision cannot be changed, and requests are not deleted.
  • Approving leave changes nothing else: attendance is not marked, and no balance or pay is calculated.

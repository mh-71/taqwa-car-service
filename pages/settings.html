Workshop information, invoice defaults, appearance, and data management.

Business Information

Invoice & Tax

Applied as the default on new Job Cards only. Existing Job Cards and Invoices keep their own historical tax value.

Invoice numbering (INV-0001…) is not configurable here — it's a fixed system convention, so it can never conflict with existing invoice IDs.

Workshop Defaults

These are reference info for your team — they are not yet enforced by the Appointments scheduler or Job Cards.

Appearance

Applies immediately — this is the same theme used everywhere in the app, including the sidebar toggle.

Data Management

Export downloads all current data as a JSON file. Import is not offered — safely validating an arbitrary uploaded file against every collection's structure before overwriting live data isn't something this app can do safely without a lot more validation work, so it's left out rather than shipped half-safe.
